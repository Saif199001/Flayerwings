About Flayer Wings

Your 24×7 Technology Partner for Digital Growth & Innovation

Who We Are

Flayer Wings is a cutting-edge technology company offering world-class solutions in web development, mobile apps, branding, AI automation, and digital marketing. We help startups and enterprises accelerate innovation, drive efficiency, and unlock new growth opportunities.

Our team thrives on creativity, collaboration, and continuous improvement — shaping a future where technology becomes a growth partner for every business.

Our Mission

"We don’t just build digital solutions — we enable digital revolutions." At Flayer Wings, our mission is to empower businesses, creators, and innovators by delivering smart, scalable, and transformative digital solutions that drive measurable impact. We exist to: Unlock the potential of ideas through cutting-edge technology Build intelligent, intuitive, and future-ready systems Serve as a 24/7 technology partner — not just a vendor, but a growth catalyst We are driven by a single goal: To turn ambition into acceleration — and innovation into success.

Our Vision

"To become the wings behind every rising idea in a digitally evolving world." We envision a world where every business — from startup to enterprise — has seamless access to innovation, creativity, and reliable digital transformation. Our Journey

2022
Founded in Delhi

2023
Launched AI Automation Tools

Expanded into AI & automation solutions to serve industries like marketing, customer support, and eCommerce.

2024
Served 150+ Clients

2025
New Horizons
